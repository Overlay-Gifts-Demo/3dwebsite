Questions owners ask

Do my guests need to download an app?

No. Guests scan the QR code and the AR camera opens in their phone browser.

Which phones does it work on?

Most modern smartphones with a camera and an up-to-date browser. We test on your phones during the demo.

How long does setup take?

It depends on how many dishes you have. Message us and we'll share a timeline for your menu.

Do I reprint QR codes when prices change?

No. Prices and dishes update on your AR menu page, and the same QR codes keep working.
